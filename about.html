Meet Dr. S.R. Radhika — Dermatologist in Malleshwaram, Bengaluru

Home > About

Dr. Radhika S.R.

MBBS, MD Dermatology Venereology & Leprosy, DNB Dermatology Venereology & Leprosy, Fellowship in Trichology

Dr. Radhika brings over 9 years of academic and clinical excellence in dermatology. After completing her MD and DNB, she pursued a prestigious Fellowship in Trichology to provide specialized care for hair and scalp disorders.

Her expertise spans across clinical dermatology, laser treatments, and aesthetic procedures. She is known for her patient-centric approach, ensuring every treatment plan is evidence-based and tailored to the individual's skin type and lifestyle.

Fluent in English, Hindi, and Kannada, Dr. Radhika ensures clear communication and comfort for patients from all walks of life in Bengaluru.

"Every patient deserves a personalised treatment plan — because no two skins are the same."

Our Clinic Story

Aura Cutisurg Clinic was established in Malleshwaram, Bengaluru, as a dedicated centre for advanced skin, hair, laser, and surgical care. Our mission is to provide dermatological solutions that are evidence-based, compassionate, and tailored to every patient.

The clinic is designed to be a calm, modern space where medical expertise meets state-of-the-art technology. We prioritize hygiene, patient comfort, and results.
