How to Choose the Right Sunscreen for Indian Skin — Expert Advice from Bengaluru

By Dr. S.R. Radhika · May 02, 2025

Why Sunscreen Is Non-Negotiable for Indian Skin

Contrary to popular belief, melanin-rich Indian skin still needs protection. While we are less prone to sunburn, we are highly susceptible to tanning, melasma, and premature aging caused by UVA rays.

SPF vs. PA Rating — What Does It Mean?

SPF (Sun Protection Factor) measures protection against UVB rays (which cause burning). PA rating measures protection against UVA rays (which cause aging and pigmentation). For Bengaluru, look for at least SPF 30 and PA+++.

The Best Sunscreen Types for Oily, Dry, and Combination Skin

For oily skin, gel-based or matte-finish sunscreens are best. Dry skin types should look for cream-based formulas with added moisturizers like hyaluronic acid. Combination skin often benefits from lightweight lotions.

5 Sunscreen Mistakes to Avoid

  • Using too little: You need two finger-lengths for the face and neck.
  • Not re-applying: Sunscreen wears off every 2-3 hours.
  • Only wearing it outdoors: UV rays can penetrate windows.
  • Skipping it on cloudy days: 80% of UV rays pass through clouds.
  • Using expired products: Active ingredients degrade over time.
