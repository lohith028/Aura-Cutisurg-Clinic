Hair Loss in Women: Causes and Treatment Options in Bengaluru

By Dr. S.R. Radhika · May 08, 2025

Why Are So Many Women in Bengaluru Experiencing Hair Loss?

Female hair loss is becoming increasingly common in urban centers like Bengaluru. Factors such as hard water, environmental pollution, high-stress levels, and dietary deficiencies play a significant role.

Common Causes of Female Hair Loss

Telogen Effluvium (stress-induced shedding), nutritional deficiencies (Iron, Vitamin D, B12), and hormonal imbalances (PCOS, Thyroid) are the leading causes. Understanding the underlying trigger is the first step toward effective treatment.

When Should You See a Trichologist?

If you notice more than 100 strands of hair loss per day, visible thinning at the partition, or a significant decrease in your ponytail's thickness, it's time to see a specialist. Dr. Radhika, who holds a Fellowship in Trichology, provides expert clinical diagnosis for hair and scalp issues.

6 Signs of Abnormal Hair Loss in Women

  • Widening of the hair partition.
  • Receding hairline at the temples.
  • Sudden patch of baldness.
  • Excessive hair on the pillow or shower drain.
  • Scalp itching or dandruff accompanying hair fall.
  • Reduced hair volume overall.
