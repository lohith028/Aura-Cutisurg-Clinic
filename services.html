Frequently Asked Questions

How many sessions does laser hair removal take?

Typically, it takes 6-8 sessions for significant hair reduction, though results vary by individual.

Is acne treatment safe for all skin types?

Yes, at Aura Cutisurg, we customize every treatment to match the specific needs of Indian skin types.

Can old tattoos be removed with laser?

Yes, our advanced lasers can effectively break down ink even from tattoos that are decades old.
