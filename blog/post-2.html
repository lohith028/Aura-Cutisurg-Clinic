5 Skin Changes You Should Never Ignore — A Bengaluru Dermatologist's Guide

By Dr. S.R. Radhika · April 18, 2025

Why Skin Changes Shouldn't Be Taken Lightly

Your skin is your body's largest organ and often reflects what's happening inside. While many changes are just part of aging or lifestyle, some can be early warning signs of more serious conditions.

5 Skin Changes That Need a Doctor's Attention

  • Changing Moles: If a mole changes color, size, or shape, it needs an immediate check.
  • Persistent Redness: Chronic redness could be rosacea or an underlying inflammation.
  • Unusual Dark Patches: Sudden pigmentation might be hormonal or sun damage.
  • Non-Healing Sores: Any spot that bleeds or doesn't heal within 3 weeks should be examined.
  • Sudden Itchiness: Generalized itching without a rash can sometimes signal internal health issues.

What Happens If You Delay Treatment?

Delaying treatment for skin conditions can lead to complications, infections, or even the progression of skin cancers. In a city like Bengaluru, where we have high sun exposure, being vigilant about skin changes is crucial.
